# Export a cluster to a paper (de-ledgering)

When: a cluster of events has at least one BRIDGE CANDIDATE that passed a confirmatory test, or the collection of LANDED and DEAD verdicts is itself a useful negative result (a table of negative results is a legitimate contribution).

Steps
1. Choose the cluster and list the events that enter it (ID, date, status). INVALID events do not enter as results; they may enter as a note on method.
2. Remove internal vocabulary: project names, pet-hypothesis names, check nicknames, role names, IDs, run times, model names in the main text.
3. Translate into the field's standard apparatus. Every claim gets its label: theorem with proof, numerical observation with method and seeds, conjecture.
4. The history of failures goes into the Discussion as motivation (why naive approaches fail), not into Results.
5. State novelty with its scope: "to the best of our knowledge, within <databases, terms, date>".
6. Appendix: scripts, environment, raw results, the exact preregistration (date frozen, commit hash if in Git).
7. Disclose AI assistance honestly and specifically: which parts, which models, and that the questions and responsibility are the author's.
8. Before submission: one review by a separate clean-context agent over the whole manuscript, with a list of claims it cannot verify.
